About PlantVision AI

AI-powered plant disease detection using image processing and deep learning.

Smart Plant Disease Detection

PlantVision AI is a deep learning based system that analyzes plant leaf images and identifies whether the plant is healthy or affected by a specific disease.

The system uses a Convolutional Neural Network trained on more than 35,000 plant leaf images across 23 different classes.

Users can upload a leaf image and receive a prediction along with the model's confidence score.
